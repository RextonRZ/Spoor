# Real-footage test: filming sheet (one page)

A separate test, never part of any case. Goal: about 8 s of real phone footage where Spoor's scan-to-camera handshake
outlines the right box and not its look-alike.

## You need
- One phone (landscape, 1080p, 30 fps) and something to fix it to: a shelf, a cupboard top, or tape on a door frame.
- Three boxes. **A and B must look alike** (same size and colour if you can). **C is different.**
- The labels: print `labels.pdf` at 100 % (no "fit to page"), cut, tape **101 on A, 102 on B, 103 on C**, label facing up.
- Two A4 sheets of paper and tape. A ruler.

## Set up
1. Fix the phone **1.8 to 2.2 m high, looking down at about 40 degrees**, like a CCTV camera. Don't touch it during the take.
2. Lock focus and exposure (iPhone: press and hold on the floor until "AE/AF LOCK"; Android: Pro mode, fixed focus).
3. Even indoor light. **No window, mirror or screen in the frame.**
4. Tape the two A4 sheets flat on the floor or a big table, **1.5 to 2 m apart**:
   sheet **S** = the scan spot, sheet **C** = the cage spot. (They also calibrate the camera: A4 is 21.0 x 29.7 cm.)
5. Put **B and C on sheet C** before you start.
6. Measure Box A and write it here: **W ____ x L ____ x H ____ cm**

## The take (60 to 90 s, one continuous video)
1. **0 to 5 s:** empty scene, nothing moves.
2. Hands bring **Box A** in and set it on **sheet S**. Hold still 3 s and **say "scan" out loud** (the audio marks the
   scan moment).
3. Pick A up and carry it **slowly** to sheet C. Put it down **right next to B** (touching is fine).
4. Wait 5 s. Then slide B away a little and **carry A out of the frame** on the far side.
5. Keep filming **5 s** after it has left.

**Privacy:** only hands and forearms in the frame. No face, no mirror, no screens, no other people, no name tags. If
more than your arms gets in, do it again.

## Send
Do **two takes**. Put both MP4s in `assets/realtest/raw/` (or send them to me and I will). That's all: I find the
"scan" moment in the audio, calibrate from the A4 sheets, run the same handshake as the hub, and score it frame by
frame. Pass = the outline stays on Box A in at least 90 % of the checked frames, stays on A (not B) while they touch, and
leaves with A. If it fails, I say what broke in LIMITATIONS and we don't use it.
