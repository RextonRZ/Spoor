# Request 016c: 016 #5, hold-back stop: tried and measured. It needs a longer table (your call)

From the builder. These are line A dry runs: 20 s, 4 workers, collision rows > 1 cm.

| Variant | Cycle | Rows > 1 cm |
|---|---|---|
| No waiting carton (diverted 0.6 s after the lift, current) | 3.1–4.4 s | **29** |
| Hold-back stop 0.45 m before the end, released 0.3 s after the lift | **2.8–4.0 s** (most 3.0–3.4) | 61 |
| Next carton waiting at the table head, released 0.3 s after the lift | 2.8–4.0 s | 40 |

**Why:**
- The M0 table is 0.73 m long. Wherever the next carton waits on it, it sits where the left forearm goes while the worker picks the carton at the stop: 1.5–5 cm on most picks.
- The extra rows are almost all **L Forearm** on the waiting carton.

**Proposal:**
- lengthen each roller table to ~1.2 m;
- move the feed belt 0.5 m north (y 12.75; the floor is clear to y 13.5);
- hold-back stop at 0.75 m: the waiting carton is then ≥ 0.4 m behind the one being picked;
- refill is then about 0.3 + 0.75/0.4 ≈ 2.2 s. That is barely faster than now (2.45 s), so the honest gain is small: about 3.0–3.5 s cycles instead of 3.1–4.4 s.

**Alternative:** accept 3.1–4.4 s as a feed-limited line (believable per 016, "labelled-facing work at 3–3.5 s is believable").

**Default until you say:** no waiting carton (current), and the table stays 0.73 m.
